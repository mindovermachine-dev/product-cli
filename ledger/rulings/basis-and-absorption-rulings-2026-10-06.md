The numbers continue `ledger/rulings/ground-and-protocol-rulings-2026-10.md`.

The earlier rulings file keeps its wording as a record; read "ground" there as "basis".

>
> **25.** A pinned basis is a new token form inside `based_on`. There is no `grounds` field.
